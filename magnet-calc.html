About Surface Gauss & Magnetic Flux

Magnetic Flux (Φ): The total magnetic flux through the pole face, measured in Webers (Wb) or milliWebers (mWb). Formula: Φ = B × A, where B is magnetic flux density and A is cross-sectional area.

Surface Gauss (Gs): Magnetic flux density at the magnet surface. 1 Tesla = 10,000 Gauss. Surface Gauss is a key parameter affecting holding force and field distribution.

Calculation Method:
• Center Surface Gauss = Br × (Pc+1)/(μrec+Pc), where Pc is the operating point and μrec is the recoil permeability
• This tool uses a simplified model; actual values require demagnetization curve analysis
• For N35–N38: μrec ≈ 1.09; N40–N50: μrec ≈ 1.07; N52–N55: μrec ≈ 1.06
• Neodymium iron boron density ≈ 7.5 g/cm³
