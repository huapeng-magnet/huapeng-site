N35 Grade

Ring Neodymium Magnets

NdFeB rings with a machined bore, so a shaft, bearing or screw can pass straight through. Commonly used as rotor cores and in magnetic couplings where the field has to wrap around a shaft.

GradeN35
ShapeRing (with bore)
Max energy product33 - 36 MGOe (263 - 287 kJ/m³)
Remanence (Br)1.17 - 1.22 T
Coercivity (Hcb / Hcj)≥ 868 kA/m / ≥ 955 kA/m
Max operating temperature+80 °C
MagnetizationRadial, axial or multipole, on request
CoatingNi-Cu-Ni, zinc or black nickel
Tolerance+/-0.05 mm (ground), +/-0.1 mm (as sintered)
