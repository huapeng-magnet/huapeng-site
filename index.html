Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about grades, coatings, lead times and ordering. Still unsure? Contact our sales team.

Higher grades (N45–N52) deliver stronger pull force in the same size — ideal for compact motors and sensors. Lower grades (N35–N42) are more cost-effective for larger, less demanding parts. Tell us your application and we'll recommend the optimal grade and a high-temperature variant if needed.

Standard options are Nickel (Ni-Cu-Ni) for general corrosion resistance and Zinc for cost-sensitive indoor use. We also provide Epoxy and Black Nickel for special environments. Coating choice depends on your operating conditions — our team will advise.

Prototypes can be turned around in as little as 3 days from just 100 pcs. Mass production typically runs 2–3 weeks depending on grade and quantity. We support both small-batch sampling and full-scale OEM supply.

Yes. Beyond discs, blocks, rings and arcs we manufacture irregular and made-to-order geometries from your drawing or specification. Upload your drawing in the contact form or configurator and we'll quote precisely.

Every shipment is accompanied by a material certificate per lot. Our production is managed under ISO 9001 and materials are RoHS & REACH compliant. Additional export documentation can be provided on request.

We ship worldwide by express courier, air and sea freight with full documentation. Our magnets are for industrial, electronic and consumer use only — not for weapon or military applications. Buyers are responsible for complying with local import and applicable dual-use regulations.

Who We Are

About Huapeng Magnetics

A neodymium (NdFeB) magnet manufacturer serving distributors, OEMs and makers across 40+ countries.

Huapeng Magnetics (Ningbo Huapeng Magnetics Co., Ltd.) has specialized in sintered NdFeB permanent magnets since 2015. We manufacture the full grade range N25–N52 and a complete shape portfolio — disc, block, ring, arc and irregular geometries — across all dimensions for motors, sensors, speakers, magnetic assemblies and consumer electronics.

With a monthly capacity of 60 tons, we turn your drawings into samples in as fast as 3 days from just 100 pcs, and deliver worldwide by express courier. Every shipment is accompanied by material certificates and, where required, export documentation.
