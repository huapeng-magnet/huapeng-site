From Raw Material to Finished Magnet

Our Manufacturing Process

Seven controlled steps ensure every magnet meets tight dimensional and magnetic specs — no shortcuts, full traceability.

01

Raw Material Prep

Precise alloy charge of Nd, Fe, B and rare-earth additions — weighed to ±0.1% for consistent chemistry.

02

Melt & Cast

Induction melting in argon atmosphere, followed by rapid solidification into ribbon or ingot form.

03

Jet-Milling

Atmosphere-controlled micro-powder production — particle size D50 ~3–4 μm for high-density pressing.

04

Pressing & Orientation

Magnetic-field-aligned compaction in a uniaxial or isostatic press to set the preferred crystal axis.

05

Sintering

Vacuum or argon furnace sinter at 1000–1100 °C to achieve >95% theoretical density.

06

Grinding & Cutting

Diamond tools slice and grind to final dimensions; hole drilling, chamfering and edge finishing as needed.

07

Coating & Inspection

Ni-Cu-Ni, Zn or black-nickel electroplating, followed by Gauss-meter testing, dimensional QA and packaging.
